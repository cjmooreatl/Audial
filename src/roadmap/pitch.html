02 The Problem fig. 01 — Solitary listening

Streaming gave us
everything. It gave us
no one to listen with.

The algorithm replaced the record shop employee. The platform replaced the DJ. We have 100 million songs and no culture around how we choose them.

Playlists are the most intimate cultural objects online — and they live in a black box. Unshareable. Unattributed. Unseen.

The person behind the recommendation has been deleted.

05 The Product Three pages. One publication.

Three pages.
One publication.

Each surface does one job, with conviction. Together they form a single editorial object — a printed schedule made interactive.

01 Home

A magazine-style feed.

Four tabs: Subscribed, On Rotation, Heavy Rotation, Drift. Asymmetric layouts, editorial headlines that rotate daily, sets from the community at every scale.

02 Channel

A curator's broadcast station.

Accent color, featured set, co-signed artists, selections. Every channel is a distinct editorial identity. Land on one and you know who you're dealing with.

03 Search + The Wire

Discovery and radio in one place.

Search across sets, channels, and tracks. The Wire runs constantly in the right column — a live stream built entirely from community-compiled tracks. File without stopping the broadcast.
