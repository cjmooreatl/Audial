The Wire is live. The feed refreshes. But the best curation products have always had a scheduled transmission — something that arrives at a specific time and says: this is what mattered this week. The Weekly Broadcast is Audial's editorial email, generated from real platform data and sent every Friday. It's a reason to come back. It's also the most shareable version of Audial for people who haven't signed up yet.

## What it looks like

- Sent every Friday at a configured time (e.g. 17:00 GMT). Subject line follows the masthead voice: `AUDIAL▪ / WEEK 47 / HEAVY ROTATION` or `THE WIRE / WEEK 47 — LATE TRANSMISSIONS`.
- The email is plain-text in spirit but HTML for rendering: Bone background, Ink text, editorial structure. No images except set covers (64×64 inline).
- Structure:
  - **Header**: `AUDIAL▪` wordmark + week number + date range.
  - **From your channels**: up to 5 sets from channels you subscribe to that were compiled this week. Each entry: cover, title, curator handle, cut count. One line.
  - **On rotation this week**: the top 3 sets platform-wide by spins in the past 7 days. Same format.
  - **A cut from The Wire**: one track from the Wire pool that's been filed into the most sets this week — the community's favorite find. Track name, artist, and the set that introduced it to the platform.
  - **Footer**: `Tune out.` unsubscribe link.

## Key details

- Opt-in only. A toggle in account settings: `RECEIVE WEEKLY BROADCAST`. Off by default for existing users, offered during onboarding for new ones.
- The email is generated by a weekly cron job. Content is computed server-side from the same feed queries powering the Home page tabs.
- "A cut from The Wire" is computed by finding the track that was filed (via `file-to-set` action) by the most distinct users in the past 7 days.
- If a user has no subscriptions, the "From your channels" section is replaced with `Top curators this week` — a fallback showing three high-spin channels.
- The email itself contains no auth gate and no tracking pixels beyond a single open-pixel for delivery confirmation (no click tracking, no heatmaps).
- The unsubscribe link is a signed one-click URL — no login required to unsubscribe.

~~~
A cron job runs every Friday at 17:00 GMT. It calls a `generateWeeklyDigest(userId)` method for each opted-in user. The method assembles the email content, renders it via a simple HTML template, and sends it using the MindStudio email integration. Batch sends to avoid rate limits. The `users` table gets a `weeklyDigestEnabled` boolean column. Unsubscribe is a `GET /unsubscribe/:token` endpoint that sets `weeklyDigestEnabled = false` — token is a signed HMAC of the userId.
~~~
